# Organic acquisition plan (static carousels)

TapKit's own output is the marketing. Make these in the app (Content Studio → Blank idea / Topic), export them at 1080×1350, and post them to Instagram and TikTok photo mode. Use the same pillars TapKit recommends to users. Never use invented results or testimonials.

| Pillar | Example carousels |
|---|---|
| Positioning | "How to discover what you should be known for" · "Common personal-brand positioning mistakes" · "Niche vs. no niche: what actually matters at zero followers" |
| Consistency with purpose | "Why posting consistently is not enough" · "A 30-minute weekly brand routine" |
| Proof over hype | "Turning one experience into five content ideas" · "Your best content is already in your notes app" |
| Learning from results | "How to learn from content performance" · "One post is not a pattern" · "Run your first content experiment" |
| Opportunities | "How to turn expertise into opportunities" · "Your portfolio should do the selling" |

Formats that suit the renderer: hook → list/checklist → comparison → myth vs fact → CTA. The landing page reuses real renderer output in its "Example output" section, labelled as an example.

Measure the marketing with TapKit's own Growth Experiments (e.g. "myth-vs-fact hooks vs question hooks", success metric: saves).
